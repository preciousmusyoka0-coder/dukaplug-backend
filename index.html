How to Order on DukaPlug

Get your food and everyday essentials delivered in a few simple steps.

1

Browse Products

Explore fast food and everyday essentials, then add your favourite items to your cart.

2

Set Up Your Profile

Open My Profile and enter your name, phone number and delivery location.

3

Review Your Cart

Check your selected items, quantities, delivery fee and total amount.

4

Choose How to Pay

Select M-Pesa or Cash on Delivery at checkout and follow the instructions shown.

5

Confirm Your Order

Confirm your details and submit your order.

6

Track Your Order

Visit My Orders to check your order information and status when available.
